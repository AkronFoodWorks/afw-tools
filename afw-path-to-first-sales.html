Path to First Sales

One sale is a start. Two makes it a business.

This page keeps track of your first few sales, step by step, so you can always see what you have done and what is next. Nothing here is graded, and there is no wrong place to be on it.

One word we use a lot: cycle

A cycle is one full trip around the same six steps that every food business repeats. It does not matter what you sell or how often you sell it — the steps are the same:

Find buyers→ Take the order→ Book the kitchen→ Make it→ Hand it over & get paid→ Line up the next one

Doing that once means it worked. Doing it twice means it was not luck. That is the whole idea of this page.

Cycle 2 — doing it again

The same six steps, one more time. This is the one that matters most, because it is the difference between one good weekend and a business. Start ticking these whenever you like, even if Cycle 1 is not finished.

Who is next

Anyone who might buy from you again, or for the first time: a repeat customer, a store that said maybe, someone who asked about catering. Keep adding to it. This list is the most valuable thing you will build here.
